# Engagement report

Use the [!UICONTROL Engagement] report to review how people engage with your marketing programs, including engagement by person and engagement trends over time.

_To view the report:_

1. In the left navigation, select **[!UICONTROL Reports]**.
1. Click the _List_ icon ( ![List icon](../assets/do-not-localize/icon-table-of-contents.svg) ) and select **[!UICONTROL Engagement]** in the _[!UICONTROL Table of contents]_ panel.

## Report tiles {#report-tiles}

The [!UICONTROL Engagement] report includes two tiles.

* **[!UICONTROL Engagement by People]** - Shows engagement across the people in your instance.
* **[!UICONTROL People Engagement Over Time]** - Shows how people engagement changes over time.

>[!NOTE]
>
>Filters are not currently available on this report. Filtering options are planned for a future release.

![Engagement report with a donut chart of engagement by people and a line chart of engagement over time](./assets/reports-engagement.png){width="700" zoomable="yes"}

You can [change the date range](./reports-overview.md#change-the-date-range) for each report tile.

Select **[!UICONTROL Share]** at the top of the report to download or schedule an export of all report data. See [_Export a report_](./reports-overview.md#export-a-report) in the Reports overview.
